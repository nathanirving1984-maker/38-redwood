Full Description

One level living with amazing views in a peaceful neighborhood — imagine no stairs from the 2-car carport to any of the living space inside. Well designed to maximize the views and fill the home with natural light, this home offers large windows and skylights over the kitchen, dining area, and bathroom. Newly refinished hardwood floors run throughout an excellent floorplan that includes a large open kitchen, dining area, a living room with fireplace, two spacious bedrooms with large closets, and a gorgeous remodeled bathroom. Don’t miss the view deck and the hot tub deck (hot tub is not operational). A newly sealed foam roof keeps the home cool in summer and warm in winter. Here’s the surprise: two large rough-framed subareas — roughly 1,100 sq ft with a 9’8” ceiling, and another approximately 1,000 sq ft on the lower level — offer real potential for expansion or an ADU (buyer to verify building potential). Extensive foundation and earthquake retrofit work was completed by the seller in 1997.
